A few places to start

Brian, these are the tools and ideas I was pointing you toward. Try them on your own work and look carefully at what comes out. I'm not asking you to learn all of this at once.

Start with the agent setup

ChatGPT and Codex. Get familiar with an agent working from a desktop or cloud computer. Give it a concrete job, let it do the work, and inspect the result.

Dots is another option to look into. OpenAI announced it September 29. Availability depends on your account and rollout.

Specialist tools

ElevenLabs for voices. Higgsfield for image and video work. Nano Banana for images. Let the agent coordinate the tools where integrations support it. Better tools still need your review.

Writing and model routing

Humanizer is worth reviewing for human-facing writing. OpenRouter is worth researching for the jury idea. Compare existing options before spending weeks building your own.

Check current plans and integrations before paying. I haven't put a fixed monthly total here because the tiers and included usage vary.
